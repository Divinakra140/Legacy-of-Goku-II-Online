  Android, Windows and Mac players all join the same games. LOCAL MULTIPLAYER: the same Wi-Fi.
  No Hamachi needed any more. Allow the game and log2_ztbridge when Windows' firewall asks.

  EARTH
- Earth reworked by hand: new forests, roads, rivers, doors, and strong foes out at the edges. Everybody walks the

- Improved by hand: the area around GOKU'S HOUSE, TRICERATOPS JUNGLE and WEST CITY.

  FIRST START
- Reading your game shows a loading bar with each step. Earth comes ready made, so it is faster than before.
- The game's music is recorded from your cartridge: RECORD WHILE I PLAY (a stand-in tune until each song is
  ready - the place you are in goes first) or RECORD ALL NOW. It never slows the game down.

  BALANCE - AS IN THE ORIGINAL GAME
- Foes keep their own strength: they no longer rise to your level. The tough ones are out at the edges - fly there.
- Levels end at 50, and each level gives 3 points (+1 strength, power and endurance). A saved hero keeps their
  level (above 50: 50) and gets the points their level now gives.
- Goku reaches SUPER SAIYAN BLUE AURA at level 40 and SUPER SAIYAN BLUE at level 50.
- Beams reach further as you level up: full length 40 levels after you learn them. Hold a beam to keep it
  going - it costs ki and ends when you let go or run out. Upward beams are drawn behind the fighter.
- Spirit Bomb hits as hard as Supernova, the Spirit Ball as hard as the Death Ball.

  CONTROLS
- SPRINT: click L3 to sprint until you stop (while flying: a boost); R2 turns sprint on and off. Keyboard: hold Shift.
- Flying: L2 (G) flies or lands, B also lands, Y boosts, and you can switch specials while you fly.
- Windows / Mac: Enter, Space, J or Z select in every menu, Esc goes back, and the mouse works in every menu
  (right click: back). Q / E change a hero's outfit on CHOOSE YOUR CHARACTER. F11: fullscreen. F3: frame rate.
- Fullscreen from the start (remembered), and the menus fill the screen at any size. OPTIONS - QUIT GAME closes it.

What this is
- The PC co-op mod rebuilt as a real game in Godot 4: no GBA engine underneath, so no screen-size limits,
  no two-games-out-of-step, no flicker. One world for everyone playing.
- EARTH, the whole planet as one map, Planet Gatorra and Planet Dunara (the hand-made adventures), or a
  RANDOM PLANET from a seed. Stories of your own made in DEV TOOLS.

First start (once)
1. Put your Legacy of Goku II (USA) .gba next to the game (or choose it when asked).
2. Start the game (LoG2Online.exe on Windows, LoG2Online.app on Mac). It reads the pictures, maps and
   enemies from YOUR ROM (about a minute) into the "imported" folder. Nothing of the game comes with
   this download.
   Mac: the first time, right-click LoG2Online.app, choose Open, then Open again.
   Windows: if "Windows protected your PC" shows, press More info, then Run anyway.

Playing together
- LOCAL MULTIPLAYER: everyone on the same Wi-Fi. One hosts, the others join.
- GLOBAL MULTIPLAYER: over the internet, with anyone on Android, Windows or Mac. Host a public game or a
  private one with a password; the others find it in the list and join.
- Go separate ways or together: each player can be in any area. Enemies are run by the host, so
  everyone sees the same enemies, the same beaten foes, the same boss.

Controls (pad or keyboard)
- Move: d-pad / stick / arrows / WASD
- A (Z or J): punch - a 4-hit chain. Hold A: the rushing strike.
- B (X or K): ki blast. Hold B: charge your beam, let go to fire.
- X (C or L): special move.  R1 (E or O): next special.  Y (V or I): transform.  L1 (F or U): item.
- Sprint: click L3 (sprints until you stop; flying: a boost), or hold Shift. R2: sprint on / off.
- L2 (G): fly / land (FREE PLAY).  START (H or Esc): menu.  SELECT (Tab or M): map.

Your fighters are saved per world (on Windows in AppData\Roaming\Godot\..., on Mac in
~/Library/Application Support/Godot/...).
