LEGACY OF GOKU 2 ONLINE - DEV TOOLS (your team's editors)
=========================================================

Open them from the main menu: DEV TOOLS. (Or start the game as:  LoG2Online.exe -- --devtools)
"DEV TOOLS tutorial v34.mp4" shows every tool in under four minutes.

New to it? HELP (top right) > FIRST MAP - STEP BY STEP walks you through your first map: a map of your
own, ground, walls, someone to talk to, a door in from Earth, SAVE, PLAY HERE. Every step ticks
itself off. HELP > A TIP shows one of many tips (one shows each time you open the tools).

Nothing needs programming. Everything your team changes is saved next to LoG2Online.exe, never in
the imported ROM data, so a new import of the ROM never loses it:

  mods\maps\          changes to the game's maps, and the maps your team makes (mods\maps\custom\)
  content\            heroes, enemies, moves, forms, blasts, missions, events, shops, loot, colours,
                      your own sprite sets (content\sprites\) (JSON text files and PNGs)
  modpacks\           mods from other people (see 5. MODS AND SHARE), switched on in MODS
  backups\            a copy of mods and content at every save (see 4. CHECK AND HISTORY)

To work as a team: TOGETHER (top right) lets everyone edit the same world at once over LAN or
Hamachi (see 6.). Or turn your work into a mod with SHARE and send the .zip.


1. THE MAP EDITOR (tab MAP)
---------------------------
The map list (top left) has:
  EARTH (THE WHOLE PLANET)   the game's world, one map, every house and cave on it
  MY MAP: ...                maps your team made (NEW MAP, or MAKE IT MY MAP)
  BUU'S FURY z-a             Buu's Fury's maps (after OTHER GAMES, see 3.)
  LEGACY OF GOKU 0-n         The Legacy of Goku's maps (after OTHER GAMES)

Moving round: right or middle mouse drag, WASD or the arrows. Wheel: zoom.
Keys: 0-4 layer, Ctrl+Z undo, Ctrl+S save, Delete deletes the chosen foe / person / door / item.
Check boxes: WALLS (red = you cannot walk there), GRID, OBJECTS.

Layers: 0 ground, 1 and 2 things on the ground (under the heroes), 3 and 4 drawn over the heroes
(roofs, tree tops). Every map cell = 5 layers + one wall bit.

Tools
  PAN      drag to move round
  TILE     paint the tile chosen on the right on the chosen layer. The list above the tiles
           switches between this game's tiles and Buu's Fury's.
  STAMP    paint a whole cell (all 5 layers and its wall) - choose it with PICK
  ERASE    take the tile off the chosen layer
  PICK     click a cell on the map: it becomes the brush (STAMP), its tile the TILE brush
  WALL     paint walls (where nobody can walk)
  GROUND   paint walkable ground
  SELECT   click a foe (red), person (blue), treasure (gold) or door (yellow); drag to move it,
           DELETE on the right to take it away
  COPY     drag a box: everything in it (all layers and walls) goes to the clipboard
  PASTE    click: the clipboard is laid down with its top-left corner there. The clipboard stays
           when you switch maps: copy from one map, paste on another (whole buildings, whole places)
  FILL     drag a box: filled with the brush
  FOE      click: a foe of that kind (the game's own enemy numbers 0-127), at its own level or the
           level set on the right
  PERSON   click: a person with a name, a sprite set and lines (they talk when you stand next to
           them and press A; one line per text box)
  ITEM     click: treasure lying there until someone takes it
  DOOR     1. drag the door's box  2. choose the map it leads to in the list  3. click where you
           arrive. Doors open at once, no loading. Make one back the same way.
  SMART    paint ground that fits: it looks at what is round each cell (grass next to a path,
           sand next to water...) and picks the game's own edge pieces. Brush size on the right.
  LINE     drag: a straight line of the chosen brush (TILE, CELL, WALL or GROUND - on the right)
  RECT     drag: a box of the brush, filled or just its edge
  THINGS   whole objects to stamp: trees, houses, rocks, fences... The list has the ones saved by
           your team; FIND THINGS IN VIEW adds everything on screen that stands on its own.
           Click to put it down (with its walls). To keep one: COPY it, then SAVE THE COPY AS A THING.
  ZONE     drag a box: a field of foes that come back - which kinds (e.g. 8,12,40), how many, their
           level and how many seconds until a beaten one comes back
  EVENT    drag a box: walking into it starts an event (choose which on the right; make events in
           the EVENTS tab). ONLY BY AN ATTACK: only a punch or a blast landing in the box starts it -
           a rock to break, a crate, a switch to hit.
  MOOD     drag a box: inside it the place feels different - DARKNESS (with a light round each
           fighter; the light size on the right), WEATHER (rain, snow, ash, dust) and its own MUSIC
           (field, cave, lair, boss, title or a song file from your music folder). A dark cave, a
           haunted wood, a snowstorm on the mountain. SELECT (click inside) to delete it.
  DOOR     (also) LOCKED DOORS: on the right before you make it - NEEDS an item (and whether the item
           is USED UP), NEEDS a flag (e.g. event:my_event), NEEDS a level, and the LOCKED TEXT shown
           when it won't open. Once opened with a used-up key it stays open (saved).
  PERSON   (also) a SHOP the person keeps, or an EVENT that starts when you talk to them

VIEW (top bar)  LITTLE MAP (click it to jump), SHOW LAYER 0-4 (hide layers to see under roofs),
                THE WHOLE WORLD (all of Earth at once with the place names - click to zoom there).
                Zoom far out with the wheel and it switches to the whole-world view by itself.
CHANGES         marks every cell your team changed (and the mods switched on) in colour.
PLAY HERE       the game right where you are looking: choose the hero, the level and whether you
                can be beaten. F10 in the game brings you back to the same spot in the tools.
                (A play-test never touches your own saved heroes and starts fresh each time.)

NEW MAP      a map of your own: name and size, it starts as one ground (the cell picked last).
             Paint it, then put a DOOR to it on EARTH.
SAVE         writes mods\maps\... (leaving a map or the editor also saves)
UNDO         the last 60 strokes

What the files look like (in case you want to script things):
  mods\maps\land_0_0.json   {"cells": {"x,y": [layer0..layer4, walk]}, "enemies_add": [[kind, set,
                            x, y, level]], "enemies_del": [[x, y]], "people_add": [{x, y, set, name,
                            lines}], "people_del", "doors_add": [{rect: [x0,y0,x1,y1], to: map key,
                            arrive: [x, y]}], "doors_del", "items": [{x, y, id, count}],
                            "items_del": [[x, y]], "zones": [{rect, kinds, count, level,
                            respawn}], "triggers": [{rect, event, attack}], "moods": [{rect,
                            dark, light, weather, music}]}  (doors_add may have need_item,
                            need_flag, need_level, locked_msg, take_item)
                            x, y in pixels for things, in tiles for cells
  mods\maps\custom\NAME.bin "LKR2", width, height (16 bit), 5 layers of 32-bit cells, a walk byte each
  mods\maps\custom\NAME.json its name, size, arrival point, song


2. THE OTHER EDITORS
--------------------
Every editor: a list on the left, the fields on the right, a preview of the sprite set.
NEW makes a new entry from a template, COPY copies the chosen one, DELETE deletes it,
+ FIELD adds a field. SAVE writes the file; the game reads it the next time it starts a world.

  HEROES    content\heroes.json   the playable heroes: name, sprite set, numbers (hp, ki, power,
            defence, speed...). NEW: a hero of your own that plays like one of the game's ("base":
            it gets that hero's moves and forms unless "inherit" is false).
  ENEMIES   content\enemies.json  every kind of foe: level, hp, attack, exp, its behaviour (the
            game's own scripts), its sprite set. Only what you change is written.
  MOVES     content\skills.json   special moves: who learns them, from which level, power, ki,
            range, its sprite set / effect
  FORMS     content\forms.json    transformations: who, from which level, how much stronger, look
  BLASTS    content\rom_specials.json  the heroes' own drawn attacks and where each part sits.
            TUNE IN THE GAME starts a world with that hero and the BLAST TUNER on:
              F9 on / off, [ ] the part, , . the attack, 1-4 the facing, arrows move the part
              (Shift: 4 pixels), Ctrl+S saves. Fire it, nudge it until it sits right, save.
  MISSIONS  content\quests.json   missions: beat foes, go somewhere, reach a level, talk to
            someone - who gives it, the text, the reward (exp, zeni, items)

  EVENTS    content\events.json   little scripts with no programming: when (talking to a person, walking
            into an EVENT box, beating a foe kind, the world starting), only if (flags, level, a mission
            done, an item), once or every time - and the steps, one under the other:
              say, choice (answers jump to labels), label, goto, if_flag, flag, give, take, zeni, exp,
              heal, quest (give a mission), finish_quest, wait, fade, warp (to a map and spot), spawn
              (foes), shop, sound, music, toast, end
            "%s" in a line is the hero's name, {var:coins} a number. A line too long for one text box is
            marked; SPLIT INTO BOXES THAT FIT cuts it up. TEST IN THE GAME plays it at once.
            STORY STEPS (for games of your own - see 7. GOOD TO KNOW
---------------
- Walls: the game's own collision is kept exactly. If you paint new things, paint their walls
  too (WALL), and check with WALLS on - red is solid.
- Foes, people and doors are matched by their spot: moving the game's own foe takes the old one
  away and puts a new one in.
- Everything is plain JSON: open the files in any text editor if the tools do not have a field.
- A broken file? Delete it (or the line) and the game uses the game's own data again.


8. MAKING A STORY):
              scenes  move (someone - or PLAYER - walks to x, y; wait or not), face, pose (any pose of
                      the sprite set: 18 = charging a beam...), camera (to a spot or a person, zoom,
                      frames), camera_back, shake, show_person, hide_person, emote (! ? ... over a head),
                      say / choice with a FACE (a sprite set shown left of the text box)
              fights  boss_fight (kind, level, name, hp x, where; WIN label, LOSE label, phases like
                      0.5:half,0.2:last - at that much health left the event jumps there, says its
                      lines, and FIGHT ON goes back to the fight). The doors stay shut until it ends.
              numbers var (set, add, sub, mul, random), if_var (=, !=, <, <=, >, >=), timer (seconds,
                      shown on screen), if_timer (ran out?)
              story   companion (a hero or a sprite set fights beside you; leave), switch_hero (play
                      as another hero - each keeps their own level), open_area / close_area (a box of
                      the map becomes walkable or a wall - a boulder smashed, a bridge built; kept
                      after saving), save, the_end (the credits)
            LINES FROM THE GAME... (on a say step): every line the game's people say, searchable -
            click one and it goes into the step with its speaker. X, Y FROM THE MAP VIEW fills a
            spot with the middle of what the MAP tab shows.
  SHOPS     content\shops.json    a shop: its name, greeting and what it sells at what price. Give it
            to a person with the PERSON tool (or open it from an event).
  LOOT      content\loot.json     what a foe kind drops: items with a chance and count, zeni, and
            whether it replaces the game's own drops
  SPRITES   your own characters:
            - IMPORT: a PNG sheet, one row per pose x direction (stand, walk, punch... facing down, up,
              right, left), frame width and height, where the feet are. GBA COLOURS and the colour
              limit make it look like the game. Left can be the right side mirrored. It becomes a new
              sprite set (4000 and up) for heroes, foes and people.
            - ANIMATIONS: open any set (the game's too) and fix each frame: move it, change the order,
              double one, delete, mirror. COPY X,Y + PASTE puts many frames at one place; MOVE BY shifts
              a pose, all its directions or the whole set. TAKE FRAMES FROM A SET shows every pose of any set as
              pictures: click the frames you want (several at once), then ADD or REPLACE. SAVE keeps it as your
              version; BACK TO THE GAME'S OWN undoes all of it.
  STATES    content\states.json   RPG-style states on a fighter for a while: poison, regeneration, slow,
            haste, weakened, rage, iron skin, blind, paralysed, ki sealed, confused - and your own.
            Fields: seconds (0 = until something takes it off), hp_per_sec / ep_per_sec (minus hurts),
            can_kill, str / pwr / end / speed (multiplied), blind, stun, no_ki, no_transform, confuse,
            form (turned into that form while it lasts), tint, aura, msg, color.
            Who gives them: events (STATE step - and take them off: off, or id "all"), moves
            ("self_state": on you; "inflict": {"state": "poison", "chance": 0.5, "seconds": 8} on the
            foes hit), foe kinds (ENEMIES + FIELD "inflict": {"state": "slow", "chance": 0.25}),
            items ("state", "cure": "all" or a list) and missions (reward "states"). Foes feel poison,
            regeneration, paralysis and slow.
  FORMS     (also) "unlock": level (at its level), reward (a mission's reward "forms" or an event's
            UNLOCK FORM step gives it - e.g. Ultra Blue, SSJ4 Limit Breaker), event (never on the
            TRANSFORM button: only the TRANSFORM step, a move's "then_form" or a state's form).
            A form can be given to any hero, not only its "who".
  MOVES     (also) BUILD A MOVE: choose a kind (beam, ball, volley, wave, dash, slash, stretch, flash,
            heal, power up), the numbers and the colour, and watch it in the preview.
  COLORS    (also) PRESETS: NIGHT, AUTUMN, SNOW, NAMEK, OLD PHOTO, EVIL, GHOST and your own
            (SAVE AS PRESET) - one click for a whole new look of a set or the tiles.

Sprite set numbers: this game's 0-299, Buu's Fury's 1000 and up, The Legacy of Goku's 2000 and up,
your colour variants 3000 and up (see COLORS), your imported sets 4000 and up (see SPRITES).

  COLORS    content\colors.json   change the colours of anything:
            - a sprite set: type its number, OPEN. Click one of its colours, choose the new colour with
              ITS NEW COLOUR - only that colour changes (Goku's orange suit blue, hair blond...). Or move
              HUE / SATURATION / BRIGHTNESS for all of it. This changes that set everywhere.
            - NEW VARIANT: a copy of a set with its own colours, a new sprite set (3000, 3001...) - a red
              Saibaman next to the green one, a dark Goku. Give its number to a foe kind (ENEMIES "set"),
              a person or a hero (HEROES "set").
            - TILES: this game's, Buu's Fury's or Legacy of Goku's tile sheet - every map using them
              changes (autumn trees, night, snow...). SHEET up / down shows more of the sheet.
            BEFORE and AFTER show the change at once; SAVE keeps it (and the game uses it).
Any of them works for heroes, foes, people and moves.


3. OTHER GAMES: BUU'S FURY AND THE LEGACY OF GOKU
-------------------------------------------------
Put your own cartridge files next to LoG2Online.exe or in a "roms" folder there (the .gba or the
.zip it came in; your Downloads folder and the folder of your LoG2 ROM are looked in too):
  Dragon Ball Z - Buu's Fury (USA)
  Dragon Ball Z - The Legacy of Goku (any version)
Then press OTHER GAMES in the map editor (the first import of the game also reads them).
Nothing of these games is shipped with LoG2 Online; they are read from your files.

  Buu's Fury     all its maps (in the map list, to look at, PICK and COPY from - or MAKE IT MY MAP
                 to copy one whole as a map of your own), all its tiles (the TILE palette) and its
                 characters and effects as sprite sets 1000+
  Legacy of Goku all its 42 maps (Kame House, Goku's house, the cities, Namek, the snow, the
                 dinosaur valley... in the map list as LEGACY OF GOKU 0-n, with their walls), all its
                 tiles (the TILE palette) and its characters as sprite sets 2000+

The other games' maps cannot be changed in place (they come from the ROM): copy what you need
onto EARTH or onto a map of your own.


4. CHECK AND HISTORY (tab CHECK)
--------------------------------
CHECK EVERYTHING looks over all your team's work and lists what will not work (red) or is probably
not what you want (yellow): doors that lead nowhere or arrive inside a wall, people or foes stuck in
walls, sprite sets, items, shops or events that are not there, events that jump to a missing label
or have lines too long for the text box, maps nobody can get into, foe kinds that don't exist,
mods that change the same cells. Choose one and GO THERE (or double click).

HISTORY: every SAVE (at most one every two minutes) keeps a copy of mods and content in "backups"
(the newest 40). RESTORE puts everything back as it was then - what you had is backed up first, so
a restore can be undone too. BACK UP NOW makes one at once.


5. MODS AND SHARE
-----------------
Main menu - MODS (or the SHARE tab): the mods you have, switched on or off, in their order (the
lower one wins where two change the same thing - the arrows move them; overlaps are listed).
ADD A MOD takes a .zip (or drop the .zip in the "modpacks" folder). Your own edits always lie over
every mod.

SHARE: name, author, version and a few words, then MAKE THE MOD FILE - your changed maps, content and
sprite sets go into modpacks_made\NAME.zip (only what you changed, never anything from the ROM).
Send it to anyone: they ADD A MOD and switch it on. Several mods stack.

The example mods come with the game (in "modpacks", switched off - switch them on in MODS and
open them in the tools to see how they are made):
  Example - Little Village       an elder with a choice event, a shop, a treasure, a field of foes
  Example - The Farmer's Quest   a mission that opens when you talk to the farmer
  Example - Shadow Goku          a new playable hero in a recoloured sprite set
  Example - Forms & States       a form as a mission reward, a move that ends in a form, poison, slow
  Example - A Story Of Your Own  a short story: cutscene, clues, a timer, a dark park, a boss with
                                 two phases, a locked door, a companion, credits (see 8.)


6. TOGETHER: EDITING AT THE SAME TIME
-------------------------------------
TOGETHER (top right): one of you presses HOST, the others type the host's address and JOIN (on the
same network the PC's address, with Hamachi the host's Hamachi IPv4; port 7778 - one up from the
game's own). Joining gives you the host's world (what you had goes to HISTORY first).
Then everyone sees the others' mouse with their name and tool, and every tile, wall, foe, person,
door, treasure, zone and event box anyone changes, as they change it. Events, shops, loot, colours,
sprite sets and new maps go across each time someone saves them. Everyone presses SAVE for their
own copy. If the host leaves, everyone keeps what they have.


8. MAKING A STORY (a single-player game of your own)
----------------------------------------------------
STORY tab: give it a TITLE and the main menu shows your story on top with 3 save slots, PLAY THE
STORY and ERASE SLOT. Fields: subtitle, hero (who you play), start_map + x, y (where a new game
starts - X, Y from the MAP tab), intro_event (an EVENTS id played once at the start), credits (the
lines THE END scrolls) and credits_music. A story keeps its own saves (not your normal heroes):
where you stood, flags, numbers, companions, opened places, who was shown or sent away.

How the game's own story feels, step by step:
  1. A cutscene: SHOW PERSON, CAMERA to them (zoom 1.5), EMOTE !, MOVE them to you, SAY with a FACE,
     CAMERA BACK.
  2. Asking around: people (PERSON tool) with an EVENT each that adds 1 to a number (VAR add), and
     IF VAR >= 3 sets a flag.
  3. A place you may only enter later: an EVENT box with "only when flags set", a locked DOOR that
     needs an item, a boulder (ONLY BY AN ATTACK box + OPEN AREA).
  4. A boss: BOSS FIGHT with a WIN, a LOSE and a phase (0.5:angry) - a SAY and a TRANSFORM at half
     health, then FIGHT ON.
  5. SAVE, and at the very end THE END.
F8 in the game: the EVENT DEBUGGER - the running event and its step, every flag, number, timer and
state. F7 (with F8 on) jumps over the step it waits on. CHECK finds steps naming people that are not
there, labels that are missing, boss phases written wrong, a story intro that is not an event.

The example mod "Example - A Story Of Your Own" (THE MISSING KEY CAPSULE, about 5 minutes) uses all
of it - switch it on in MODS, then look at its events in the EVENTS tab.

Good to know: the story camera zoom is for one player (with two on one PC it only moves);
companions don't get hurt; the game's own story scenes can't be read from the ROM (they are
compiled code), but every line its people say is in LINES FROM THE GAME.
