LoG2 ONLINE - v44.5 "EMPTY EARTH" (experimental)
================================================

This is a snapshot of Earth while it is being rebuilt. It is not the official v45 release.

What is in it:
  - The new shape of the world: one big continent, with its mountains, deserts, grassland, rivers and snowy north
    painted in.
  - The SOUTHERN CONTINENT built, with its places set in it, as an example of a finished part of the world.
  - Every other place of the game parked at the edge of the map (the strip on the right and the dark area along the
    bottom), ready to be dragged into the world with the map editor (DEV TOOLS - MOVE).
  - Roaming foes all over. The story's events are not placed yet.

Play around in it, or open the map editor and build your own Earth from it. See "Earth Editor Guide.txt" and
"DEV TOOLS.txt".

New in the map editor since v44: BIOME (natural ground, any brush size), the EDGE brushes under SMART (beach,
lake, waterfall, foam), ROCK (rocks to smash that drop food and ki), LASSO for MOVE and COPY, and painting while
zoomed right out.

The first start reads your game again (the importer has new parts) and lays this Earth in once - give it a minute.
You need your own Legacy of Goku II (USA) game file, as always. Nothing from the game is included.
