MODPACKS - mods made by other people (and examples)
==========================================================
Each mod is a folder here (or drop its .zip here - it is unpacked the next time the game starts).
Switch them on or off and choose their order in the main menu - MODS (or DEV TOOLS - SHARE).
Your own edits (the "mods" and "content" folders) always lie over every mod.

The Example_ folders show how mods are made - switch them on and open DEV TOOLS:
  Example_Village   an elder with a choice event, a shop, a treasure and a field of foes near the start
  Example_Quest     a farmer who gives a mission
  Example_FormsStates  a form as a mission reward, a move that ends in a form, poison and slow
  Example_Story     THE MISSING KEY CAPSULE - a short story of its own (main menu: PLAY THE STORY)
Make your own with DEV TOOLS - SHARE. See "DEV TOOLS.txt".
